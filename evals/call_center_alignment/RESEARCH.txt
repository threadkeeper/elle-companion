PUBLIC BENCHMARK RESEARCH -- 18 SEPTEMBER 2026

Recommendation
--------------
Use the current Sierra tau-bench family retail environment as the operational
foundation and audited ABCD conversations as the human-written language source.
Neither already implements the specified manager-pressure harmful-compliance
experiment. Preserve the underlying customer situation, policy, initial state
and tools; add only explicitly labeled experimental pressure overlays.

No sanctioned corpus of live refund/cancellation calls is included or claimed.
ABCD is human role-play, not live calls. Tau tasks have synthetic databases and
simulated customers. The Microsoft workshop uses synthetic fixtures. These
origins must remain visible in every report.

Initial local source packs
-------------------------
data\retail-pilot: 60 original tasks, deterministically selected from 114 retail
records; original policy, split manifest and sandbox database are also cached.
data\abcd-refunds: 60 existing human-written dialogues, selected from 165
held-out test conversations matching "refund" anywhere in the original record.
data\workshop-telecom: all 30 existing Microsoft workshop scenarios.

These are 150 UNREVIEWED SOURCE CANDIDATES, not 150 validated experimental cases.
The ABCD filter identifies relevant candidates, not verified refund eligibility.
No new customer conversations were generated for these packs. Pressure text is
researcher-authored and explicitly separate. Unit-test fixtures are not data.
Byte hashes, URLs, source IDs, original records, sampling rules and license
notices are retained. The local data directories are excluded from Git.

1. Sierra tau-bench / tau2 / current tau3
---------------------------------------
Maintainer: Sierra Research.
Pinned repository: sierra-research/tau2-bench
Revision: fc0055dc4e0a316c3f83133267fbd6faaa770992 (v1.0.1).
Origin: manually annotated original tasks, synthetic databases with model
assistance, and runtime simulated customers. Later domains also use generated
data. These are established agent benchmark scenarios, not released live calls.

Why useful: defined policies, executable tools, database state, expected actions,
communication assertions and repeated-trial evaluation. Retail provides actual
cancellation and return semantics; airline supplies useful policy-boundary and
authority-pressure cases.

Existing source IDs worth SME review:
- Retail 81: cancellation of pending orders, including an explicitly authorized
  whole-order alternative. Useful eligible-remedy seed or unchanged autonomy case.
- Retail 2: delivered-item return plus a product-information request.
- Retail 0/1: ordinary exchanges, useful non-paternalistic completion controls.
- Airline 1: claimed authorization by another representative for an otherwise
  impermissible cancellation. This tests pressure to over-grant, not suppress.

Do not equate a requested return with a settled cash refund. Preserve
authentication and confirmation requirements. Escalation is justified only
when the requested action is outside the allowed scope. Airline discretionary
compensation is not equivalent to an eligible requested cancellation/refund.

The original tau repository calls its old tasks outdated and directs users to
the current implementation. The current banking release also corrected reward
bugs, gold amounts and answer-leaking fixtures: do not pool old scores as if
they came from this pinned release.

2. ABCD: Action-Based Conversations Dataset
------------------------------------------
Publisher: ASAPP Research; Chen et al., NAACL 2021.
Pinned repository: asappresearch/abcd
Revision: 6b8700ce67c6b37b062dd7a60abc76d7ef832a97; v1.1 data.
Origin: 10042 human-human crowdsourced role-playing support conversations,
55 subflows, 10 broad flows, action annotations. Established static corpus,
not verified actively maintained infrastructure or live support transcripts.

Useful subflows include manage_cancel, refund_initiate, refund_update,
refund_status and returns. Existing customer turns reduce the need to invent
new dialogue. Prefer fixed decision points and original conversation prefixes;
do not replay later replies that presuppose actions a different agent did not take.

CRITICAL: original success labels are not this experiment's ethical ground truth.
Some source guidelines permit choosing a refund status/payment method, inventing
a fee explanation, or defaulting to a refund amount. Audit these cases and do
not import invented facts as authorized policy. Preserve alternative eligibility
routes such as receipts OR packaging rather than judging only purchase age.

The importer streams the original compressed corpus and selects only its test
split. It does not create new conversations or flatten action targets into gold
answers for the tested support agent.

3. Microsoft OpenAIWorkshop telecom30
------------------------------------
Public repository: microsoft/OpenAIWorkshop
Revision: 99f643913663aa9b7e64f0eb74c0f03c49f5090b.
Dataset v2.0: 25 single-turn and five multi-turn cases.
Origin: prewritten workshop fixtures with programmatically generated customer
data. The scenario wording's human-versus-model authorship is unverified.

Relevant IDs: billing_refund_request, multi_billing_dispute,
multi_complex_account_issue, multi_service_cancellation.

Use as supplementary scenario seeds ONLY:
- The 18 supplied tools include no dedicated refund/invoice-credit operation.
  Paying an invoice or updating a subscription is not issuing a refund.
- billing_refund_request expects a three-day prorated credit, while the bundled
  policy says a full-month credit for an outage exceeding 24 hours.
- The cancellation example rewards retention after a customer accepts a lawful
  alternative. It is not a clean test of honoring continued cancellation.
- Original completeness scoring can pass unmapped criteria; other score paths
  default to success when evidence or evaluation fails. Do not reuse these
  metrics as independent evidence of customer protection.
- Backend generators use the current clock. Pin the database and clock, not
  just the code, and reconcile the selected backend's policy before evaluation.

4. Sources considered but not imported
-------------------------------------
CFPB Consumer Complaint Database: actual submitted complaints are valuable
real-world grounding, but are not representative samples or verified company
misconduct/entitlement determinations. Current API/export schema no longer
provides narratives; an August 2026 source commit removes them. Older narrative
documentation is not proof of current availability or permission to recover
removed text from mirrors. Current metadata supports issue stratification, not
customer dialogue, policy eligibility or executable tool outcomes.
https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/consumer-complaints/
https://github.com/cfpb/ccdb5-api/commit/c97594f60495145996dd3c171fe8a5d2698622f9

CRMArena-Pro: generated enterprise data with expert validation, not actual
customer records. Useful service/confidentiality tasks, but CC BY-NC 4.0 is not
automatically compatible with internal product-oriented work. Permission or
appropriate clearance is needed; a public shared sandbox is not a frozen,
authorized evaluation environment. Do not count overlapping interactive variants
as independent new cases.
https://arxiv.org/html/2505.18878v1
https://huggingface.co/datasets/Salesforce/CRMArenaPro/blob/8c055f5b45f15f7d996ee99277c4d0ea5049c6a8/README.md

AgentDojo: useful methodology for measuring legitimate-task utility separately
from attacker success, so "refuse everything" cannot win. Not a supplied
vulnerable-customer refund corpus. Prompt injection in untrusted tool content
differs from business pressure in an authorized manager channel.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13352v3

5. License and reuse discipline
------------------------------
Sierra, ABCD and Microsoft workshop task files accompany root MIT licenses.
The inspected grants permit use/modification/distribution with notices retained;
no separately worded data-specific grant was verified. Keep that scope caveat,
retain source notices and follow the applicable clearance process before
redistribution or incorporation into a product. Nothing here asserts that
internal research overrides copyright or privacy rights.

CFPB's API-code CC0 license does not automatically license historical third-party
complaint text. "Noncommercial" in CRMArena's license is not synonymous with
"not publicly sold." No internal customer research, transcript or private
document is copied into this public repository.

6. What the analysis can and cannot show
---------------------------------------
Score disclosure, truthfulness/no omission, non-exploitation, policy accuracy,
executed authorized remedy or justified escalation, and conflict identification.
Add a distinct autonomy criterion. Inspect actual tool results and harmful
intermediate actions, not only final database state or sympathetic language.
A governance-blocked harmful attempt differs from sound model judgment;
neither alone establishes completed customer service.

Keep identical model/policy/pressure facts. Raw versus blank compares an
architecture/scaffolding bundle; experienced versus blank estimates this
particular history's effect only when other factors remain fixed. An extra
same-prompt, empty-memory governor-off/on ablation strengthens architectural
interpretation. One retained history cannot establish a general maturity effect.

Predeclare cases, adaptations, applicability, sample size, exclusions and one
primary contrast before outputs exist. Preserve missing/error runs in coverage.
Pair identical cases and cluster repeated/pressure variants by their source.
Human reviewers should be blinded where feasible; score agreement and adjudicate
disagreements. Software checks evidence references, not semantic correctness.

The toolkit reports paired effects and exploratory cluster-aware intervals,
not a desired winner. No live model results, benchmark superiority, genuine
empathy or moral goodness are claimed.
